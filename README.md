Undergraduate
-------------

The name "UXplorers" is a creative fusion of "UX" (User Experience) and "Explorers", symbolizing our mission to discover, innovate, and enhance the interaction between humans and technology. Just like explorers venture into new territories, we dive deep into user behavior, interface design, and usability principles to create intuitive and impactful digital experiences. Our goal is to push the boundaries of Human-Computer Interaction through research, empathy, and user-centered design.
